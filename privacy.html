01Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Math Camp (“the app”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use it. Math Camp is a math practice app for learners of all ages. We designed it to work fully without an account and without sending personal information to us.

02Information the app handles

The following information is created and kept only on your device. We never receive it and cannot see it.

  • Learning progress. Your best score for each task, stars earned, number of attempts and the date of your last attempt. This powers the Statistics screen and your progress through the camp.
  • App preferences. Your chosen language, whether sound effects are on, and whether you have completed the welcome tour.

Math Camp does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera, and it does not require you to create an account.

Good to know: We don’t use analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking tools, so there is nothing to opt out of.

03Purchases and subscriptions

Some content in Math Camp is available through Premium (monthly, yearly or lifetime). Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store.

  • We never see or store your payment details, Apple ID or billing information.
  • The app only asks Apple whether you own Premium, so that it can unlock content.
  • You can manage or cancel subscriptions anytime in your Apple ID settings.

Apple’s handling of your purchase data is described in Apple’s Privacy Policy.

04Sharing and third parties

We do not sell, rent, trade or share your information, because we don’t have it. Math Camp contains no third-party advertising or analytics services. The only outside service involved is Apple’s App Store, for purchases as described above.

05Minors

Math Camp does not collect personal information from anyone, including minors. The app has no accounts, no ads, no social features and no user-generated content. If you are a parent or guardian and have questions, please contact us.

06Storage, retention and deletion

Your progress and preferences stay on your device until you remove them. To delete everything, simply delete the app from your device. All locally stored data is removed with it. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing to request from us.

If you use device backups (such as iCloud Backup), Apple may include the app’s data in them according to your device settings. That is controlled by you and Apple, not by us.

07Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or export your personal data. Since we do not collect or store personal data on our servers, these rights are fulfilled by design: your data is yours and lives on your device. If you have any question about your rights, please get in touch.

08Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above. Significant changes will be noted in the app’s release notes.
